### 🛡️ Professional Overview

I am a **Security Transformation Analyst** at **Accenture**, specializing in **Cloud Security Architecture**, **Cloud Security Posture Management (CSPM / CIEM)**, and **Threat Modeling as Code**. I collaborate with enterprise clients to transform multi-cloud defense postures, establish robust Policy-as-Code guardrails, and engineer automated security pipelines bridging developer speed with stringent regulatory governance.

- ☁️ **Cloud Security & Compliance**: Auditing and hardening multi-cloud environments (AWS & Azure) against CIS Benchmarks, NIST CSF, ISO/IEC 27001, and Spanish ENS (Esquema Nacional de Seguridad).
- 📐 **Threat Modeling & Architectural Reviews**: Applying STRIDE and PASTA methodologies early in the SDLC using programmatic Threat-Modeling-as-Code and MITRE ATT&CK Cloud Matrix mapping.
- ⚙️ **DevSecOps & Autonomous Observability**: Engineering serverless telemetry pipelines, admission controllers, and automated incident containment playbooks with Python, Go, and Terraform.
- ☸️ **Container & Kubernetes Defense**: Enforcing Kubernetes Pod Security Standards (PSS Restricted), CycloneDX SBOM vulnerability scanning, and supply-chain integrity.
